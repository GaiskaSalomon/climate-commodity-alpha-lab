## 1. Hypothesis

Weather and extreme climate-risk variables contain information about future commodity returns not captured by price momentum and volatility alone. Specifically:

- Temperature anomalies predict natural gas and agricultural commodity returns.
- Hurricane risk proximity predicts crude oil and natural gas returns around the Gulf of Mexico.
- Combined features improve Information Coefficient versus price-only baselines.
